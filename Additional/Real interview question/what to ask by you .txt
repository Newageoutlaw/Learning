WHAT TO ASK AT THE END OF THE INTERVIEW TO THE MANAGER OR INTERVIEWER 

- Ask about project 
= I would like to know about the project, if possible please share the client name
= May I know about the project ?
= Is it an onshore client or offshore client?

- Ask about team 
= What would be the team size?
= is this an invidual role or an opportunity to work with the team?
= What is the bandwidth of the team?
= What is the team size? 
= How is the work culture in the team or organization? 

- Ask about nature of the project/domain
= I would like to know the nature of business of the client
= What tools are bring used in the project?
= What is the Domain of the project?
= Is the role aligned with Devops team or Management team?

- Responsibilities and expectations from a candidate 
= If I get selected, what will be expected from me? (What are your expectations from a candidate for this position?)
= What will be the key strength in a candidate you are looking for?
= My roles in that project./ What would be the roles and responsibilities  for this role? 
= What are your expected skills for this position ?
= Is there any certification required?
= Once the position is filled, how soon a candidate will receive the asset and credentials? 
= What are your expectation in handson experience with certain tools? 
= What would be the day to day activities for the position?
= What type or kind of tools are being used in the project?


QUESTIONS TO BE ASKED TO THE HR
- What is the notice period of this organization?
- How many rounds would be there and with whom? 
- What will the shift timings?
- Is it a contract to hire position or fulltime permanent role?
- If this position is contract to hire, what will be the duration of the contract?
- Will there be any relocation support or reimbursment? 
- What will be my job location? 
- Is it hybrid or onsite or remote? 
- Can you share details about the organization or tell me more about the organization?

QUESTIONS TO BE ASKED IN FINAL HR ROUND (SALARY DISCUSSION)
- What is the notice period of this organization?
- Is there any annual bonus?
- Is there any signing bonus?
- What are the appraisal cycle in the organization? What are the KPI matrix for that?
- What will be the fixed and variable in the pay?
- What are the additional benefits apart from CTC? 
- What are the leave criteria and promotional bandwidith? 
- What are the medcial benefits or what are the medical perks offered by the organization?
